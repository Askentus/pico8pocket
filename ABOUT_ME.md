# About Me and Pico8Pocket

Hello, everyone!

I am happy to introduce the alpha version of a PICO-8 emulator for the compact
console we all love: the Analogue Pocket.

I would be very grateful for your feedback—and for not judging my creation too
harshly. I am not a programmer, although I do work in the IT industry.

I used OpenAI Codex, and in just a couple of days I managed to achieve the
first working results.

Celeste was the main target game during development. As far as my gaming skills
allowed me to test it, the game is already quite comfortable to play on the
Analogue Pocket.

That said, this is still an alpha release, so please do not expect it to be
problem-free. Issues will definitely appear. If you tell me about them, I will
try to fix them whenever I can—or whenever I manage to write the right prompt,
of course. ;)

Anyway, I wish you a wonderful day and a great mood.
